Primary Finding
Evidence-LoE alignment is measurably stronger for LEO missions than Mars. The Spearman ρ trajectory shifts toward zero as mission complexity increases, indicating that the Board's LoE scores are least supported by documented evidence at the mission profiles where it matters most.
Portfolio Structure
A majority of risks share the same evidence strategy: Type I (Human Spaceflight) + Type VII (Expert Consensus). The portfolio is evidence-strategy homogeneous across radically different health domains, reflecting NASA's reliance on direct astronaut data plus expert review.
Tier 1 Null
Primary tier-1 hypothesis (evidence volume predicts LoE strength) rejected at portfolio level. Sub-group differences emerge in DRM-stratified tier-2 analysis, where LEO-specific correlations differ from Mars-specific ones.

Network Analyses of the Risk Portfolio
This portfolio is modelled as a network so its structure can be examined, not just its contents. Each risk is represented by the fingerprint of its evidence base and Level-of-Evidence ratings, and risks with similar fingerprints are linked; network-science methods then reveal how the 32 risks cluster into groups, which risks anchor each group, and which sit at the structural extremes. The driving question: does the portfolio's evidence base organise into a few coherent strategies, and where are the leverage points at which a single research investment could strengthen many risks at once? Open any card below to explore a result alongside its plain-language interpretation.

Reading the App
The Evidence Network places eight evidence-type diamond anchors at fixed corners; risks are force-pulled toward the types they cite most. Click any evidence-type diamond in the network to show only the risks that cite it, then click a risk for its full profile. The DRM Gradient tab shows the Spearman ρ trajectory plus the LoE × DRM distribution. The Topology tab visualizes the portfolio's community structure and network metrics. The Reference Guide defines every LoE level, evidence type, DRM, and disposition category. Use the top-center selector on the network to recolor by a specific DRM × Outcome.

How this panel is sourced ▾
The evidence-type counts below are pulled from the entire Risk Summary Package (the whole-risk corpus), so the bars are identical no matter which outcome is selected. The Level of Evidence, rationale, disposition, likelihood and consequence shown further down are recorded separately for each outcome track (Operations / OPS and Long-Term Health / LTH) and for each Design Reference Mission.
Evidence by JSC-66705 Type
Keyword citations across the 8 evidence categories, recorded at the risk level for the whole Risk Summary Package. These counts describe the package as a whole and are not split by outcome - the same evidence-type profile underlies both operations (OPS) and long-term health (LTH), so the bars do not change with the outcome selector. The OPS-versus-LTH distinction, each with its own disposition, likelihood, consequence and rationale, is shown per Design Reference Mission in the posture table below, where every selected cell is labelled OPS or LTH.
